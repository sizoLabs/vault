# humans.txt - A manifesto made by and for humans

## Preamble
This site is not just code,
nor just technology.
It is a place crafted with
hands, heart, and the intention
of connecting with other people.
It is a space made to be used,
felt, and lived by humans.

## I. On humanity
We know that behind every click there is
a story, behind every screen there is
a person, behind every action there is
a feeling. We do not want to see
people as numbers, nor users as
customers, nor technology
as something that controls us.

## II. On love
We do not see love as something fleeting,
it is more of a constant choice:
preferring real connection over
logic, preferring empathy
over indifference, preferring well-being
over addiction. This site does not exist
to control, it exists to serve.

## III. On kindness
Kindness is not weakness, it is courage.
It is choosing to be clear, not confusing.
It is choosing to be inclusive, not exclusive.
It is choosing to respect, not to take advantage.
We do not design to grab attention,
we design to make people feel good.

## IV. On the future
The future is not written solely by
algorithms, we write it with the decisions
we make now.
We believe in a technology that:
- Asks before assuming.
- Listens before responding.
- Respects before collecting data.
- Includes everyone, not just some.

## V. On silence and pauses
In a world that always wants more,
we offer space to breathe.
In an environment that always rushes,
we offer a moment to think.
Because creativity, connection,
and truly human ideas only
are born when one truly rests.

## VI. On responsibility
We are not only responsible for what we create,
but also for what we allow to happen
with what we create. We assume
responsibility above our
creations.

## VII. Our promise
We promise to remember that:
- The numbers behind the metrics
are real people.
- Traffic is not statistics,
it is trust.
- Metrics are not everything.

## VIII. Call to action
If this manifesto resonates in your mind,
do not leave it forgotten. Live it.
Demand a minimum of humanity from the
tools you use every day.
Every time you have to decide,
ask yourself: "Does this respect humanity?"
And if it does not, build something that does.

## Epilogue
This site is human.
It is not perfect, but it is authentic.
It is not immortal, but it is meaningful.
And at the end of the day, when the
servers shut down and the algorithms
stop working, what will remain is
the only thing that has always remained:
us.
